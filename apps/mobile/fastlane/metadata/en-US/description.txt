AgentStart is a workspace for running coding agents in parallel.

Pair the app directly with an AgentStart daemon to keep agent sessions, isolated Git worktrees, terminals, files, diffs, pull requests, and live activity together on your iPhone or iPad.

Use AgentStart Mobile to:

- Open and switch between workspaces and agent sessions.
- Read and interact with persistent terminals, files, and browser previews.
- Review diffs, stage changes, commit work, and manage pull requests.
- Resume agent history and inspect activity and provider usage.
- Review daemon notifications while connected and open the relevant workspace directly.
- Add widgets for ChatGPT, Claude, and token usage.

AgentStart does not provide or sell agent-provider accounts. Authentication, model access, and usage limits remain under the control of each provider configured on your paired daemon host.
